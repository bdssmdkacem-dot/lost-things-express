# Train Compartment — Visual Reference

Status: approved visual direction, based on the two user-provided reference images (2026-10-09).

## Primary reference: classic green-and-walnut railway compartment

Use the first image as the primary reference for the playable/searchable compartment environment:

- Long, narrow passenger carriage with a strong central vanishing point and eye-level camera.
- Dark polished walnut paneling, carved wood trim, and a wood ceiling with visible structural beams.
- Deep emerald/forest-green ceiling insets, curtains, and patterned carpet runner.
- Warm brass/gold fixtures, wall sconces, overhead luggage racks, and a modest central chandelier.
- Upholstered bench seating along the wall; wooden luggage/trunks and small discoverable objects near the floor.
- Moody, warm, cinematic lighting with realistic material textures and restrained highlights.
- The space should feel like a detailed mystery-game scene: readable silhouettes, layered props, and small clues that reward close inspection.

## Secondary reference: luxurious vintage railway lounge

Use the second image as a complementary reference for richness and atmosphere, not as a replacement for the primary layout:

- Deep burgundy/red tufted upholstery, dark polished wood, brass details, and warm table lamps.
- Tall windows framed by heavy curtains; repeated lamps and seats create depth along the carriage.
- Rich amber lighting and an elegant, slightly mysterious period-train mood.

## Consistency rules

1. Preserve the first image's green, walnut, and brass palette as the default compartment identity.
2. Borrow burgundy upholstery and lamp warmth from the second image selectively for alternate carriages, seating details, or lighting accents.
3. Prefer believable period-railway construction and tactile materials over flat, cartoon-like decoration.
4. Keep the camera composition legible for gameplay: central aisle, strong perspective, and interactable clues that remain visually distinct.
5. Treat both images as approved art-direction references for future compartment assets and UI presentation.
